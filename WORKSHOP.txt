[b]Type Demand & Risk Stars — WRT fork[/b]
Demand outlines use configurable item-type colors. Container contents use one configurable corner-hint color.
Includes trashcan contents and a contents hint on the fixed bin. A scrolling checkbox dock below the trashcan shares the advanced filter; active manual selections replace customer demand, and Clear restores it.
Independent, always-on pixel stars on all native item views: yellow = stolen, green = contraband, red = both.
Colors, star size, thickness, corner rounding and fill are configurable in the game's native Mods settings.
Demand/filter hotkeys do not disable permanent risk stars.
Based on KCN-judu's Demand Highlight (Apache-2.0). Disable the original and other overlapping highlight mods.
Built and scanner-checked against supplied real game references labelled VERSION 100-Rev13. Gameplay validation remains pending. Native drag/stack-preview stars are included.
